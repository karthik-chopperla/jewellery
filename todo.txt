6. Advanced Jewellery Price Calculation Engine
This should be a dedicated calculation module because jewellery billing is different from ordinary retail billing.

Example calculation:
Component	Example
Net gold weight	10 grams
22K gold rate	₹10,000/gram
Gold value	₹1,00,000
Making charges	₹10,000
Stone charges	₹5,000
Subtotal	₹1,15,000
GST at 3% (illustrative)	₹3,450
Final amount	₹1,18,450

Support:
- Fixed making charge per gram.
- Percentage-based making charge.
- Fixed making charge per item.
- Wastage percentage.
- Wastage weight.
- Different calculation methods by product category.
- Stone and diamond pricing.
- Custom deductions.
- Multiple tax configurations.
- Configurable rounding rules.
Actual tax treatment should be configurable and verified against applicable Indian GST rules.
